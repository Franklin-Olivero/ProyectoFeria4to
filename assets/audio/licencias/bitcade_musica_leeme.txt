MADE WITH BITCADE
-----------------
Bitcade is a retro synth for chiptune music and a sound effect generator, with four
engines: chip, 8-bit computer, 16-bit FM and sampler (VST3 plugin and standalone app,
Windows). All instruments in these tracks come from its factory presets.

Get Bitcade: https://schniersky.itch.io/bitcade
